# Data Deletion Instructions

**Last updated:** 1 June 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your personal data from **[YOUR BUSINESS NAME]**'s WhatsApp-based invoice processing service ("the Service").

You have the right to request deletion of your data under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

---

## What Data We Hold About You

When you use the Service, we may hold the following information about you:

- Your WhatsApp phone number
- Messages you have sent to our bot, including text and invoice images
- Invoice data we have extracted from images you sent (vendor names, amounts, dates, GSTINs, line items)
- Account configuration data you provided (business name, GSTIN, Tally configuration)
- Diagnostic logs containing your phone number and message metadata

A full description of the data we collect and how we use it is in our [Privacy Policy][def].

---

## How to Request Deletion

You can request deletion of your data in either of two ways:

### Option 1 — Through WhatsApp

Send the message **`DELETE MY DATA`** (case-insensitive) to our bot on WhatsApp.

The bot will reply with a confirmation prompt asking you to confirm the deletion request. Reply **`CONFIRM DELETE`** to proceed.

Once confirmed, your deletion request is logged immediately. Deletion is completed within the timeline described below.

### Option 2 — Through Email

Send an email to **prakharsuhalka25@gmail.com** with the following information:

- **Subject:** Data Deletion Request
- **Body:** Your full name, the WhatsApp phone number associated with your account (with country code), and a statement that you are requesting deletion of your personal data.

We will acknowledge your email within 48 hours and complete the deletion within the timeline described below.

---

## What Gets Deleted

When you request deletion, we delete the following:

- All invoice images you have sent
- All extracted invoice data we have on file for you
- Your account configuration data (business name, GSTIN, Tally setup)
- Diagnostic logs containing your phone number
- Backups containing your data, on the next scheduled backup rotation (within 30 days)

---

## What Is Retained

Some data must be retained even after a deletion request, for legal and regulatory reasons:

- **Audit trail of the deletion request itself**, including the date, requestor identifier, and confirmation — retained for 3 years to demonstrate compliance with the DPDP Act.
- **Anonymised, aggregated usage statistics** (e.g., total invoices processed per month across all users) that do not identify you.
- **Records required by Indian tax law**, in cases where we have issued you a paid invoice for the Service. Such records are retained for 7 years from the financial year of the transaction, as required under the Income Tax Act, 1961.

These retained records do not contain the contents of invoices you sent, your business data, or any data we extracted on your behalf.

---

## Timeline

- **Acknowledgement of request:** within 48 hours.
- **Deletion of active data** (databases, object storage, application caches): within 7 days.
- **Deletion from backups:** within 30 days, on the next scheduled backup rotation.
- **Deletion confirmation sent to you:** within 30 days, via the same channel you used to request deletion.

---

## What Happens After Deletion

Once your data is deleted:

- You will no longer be able to use the Service with the same WhatsApp number unless you sign up again.
- Any in-progress invoice extractions will be cancelled.
- We will not be able to recover your data, including past extracted invoices. If you need a record of past invoices for your own use, export them from your TallyPrime instance before requesting deletion.
